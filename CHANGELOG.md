# Changelog

Version Release Notes

## 0.9.0 - 2026-11-27

- Motion folder in the volume and raytrace styles, each part of the male moving rigidly with its bone
- Posed bone in the surface style ending cleanly at each joint in any pose

## 0.8.0 - 2026-11-26

- Import, playing a clip made for another skeleton on the male, from a GLB, glTF, FBX or BVH file chosen with the button or dropped on the page
- Save, downloading an imported clip as a file for the motion library
- Idle, walk, jog, jump and reach out from Quaternius's Universal Animation Library in the Motion folder
- Idle, walking, jogging, jump, big jump, waving, sitting idle, goalkeeper idle and batter on deck from Mixamo in the Motion folder

## 0.7.0 - 2026-11-26

- Handles at the wrists and ankles, posing the male by dragging a hand or a foot, each joint turning within a body's natural range
- Poses dragged by hand kept over the motion playing, a raised hand staying raised over the walk, until Reset
- Copy pose, putting the pose drawn on the clipboard as a pose file for the motion library

## 0.6.0 - 2026-11-26

- Anatomical position, breathing and walk in place in the Motion folder, the breathing and the walk playing over and over
- Crossfades between motions, half a second long, from wherever the body stands
- Play, Speed and Step, playing or holding a clip, setting how fast it plays and moving it on a frame at a time
- Layers folder, mixing poses and the breathing over the motion, each by its own weight
- Motion named in the page's address, opening the page on it

## 0.5.0 - 2026-11-26

- Motion folder in the splats style, each splat moving with the male's flesh and each limb taking its own splats with it

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-25

- Cuts on the posed body, each the same section of the anatomy in any pose
- Caps on the posed body's cuts, each limb's section moving with the limb
- Depth shell on the posed body, under each limb's own skin

## 0.3.1 - 2026-11-25

- Posed skin unbroken where the arms, hands and thighs part from the body
- Posed hands, knees and feet drawn whole in every pose
- Raised thighs drawing the skin of the groin after them
- User interface improvement

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-24

- Motion folder, posing the male in the surface style: as scanned, standing, T-pose, arms out, arms forward, knee up or sitting
- Joints switch, drawing the skeleton over the posed body

## 0.2.7 - 2026-11-24

- User interface and app improvements
